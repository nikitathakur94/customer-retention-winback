# Demonstration order

## Ten minutes

0–1: Overview, source dates and reconstruction disclosure. 1–3: Customer health and eligibility. 3–5: Risk model, prevalence baseline and ablation. 5–7: Customer explorer, reconcile purchase days and value; deliberately reveal separate future outcomes. 7–9: Win-back studio, capacity zero then a small list, unknown consent and hypothetical incentive math. 9–10: Campaign lab, assigned-customer comparisons, uncertainty and the next experiment.

## Thirty-minute technical walkthrough

0–5: Source manifest, hashes, duplicate policy and missing prices. 5–10: Generated snapshot SQL, exact-cutoff fixture, eligibility and immature labels. 10–17: Train-only preprocessing, validation split, calibration, model freeze and test evaluation. 17–22: Segment precedence, tie-breaking, suppression and scenario cost formula. 22–27: Hillstrom random assignment, excluded outcome predictors, IPW policy value and noisy Qini. 27–30: Reproduction commands, dbt and integration checks, remaining source limits and original-project boundary.
