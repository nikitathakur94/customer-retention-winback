# Business brief and proposed marketing requirements

Public-data reconstruction inspired by Nikita Thakur's Factspan/Macy's business problem. This is not original Macy's data, code or measured company impact.

Decision owner: proposed retention marketing analyst. Investigate which observed prior buyers are unlikely to purchase in the next 28 days and compare simple recency rules with purchase and digital features. The operational output is a capacity-limited local candidate list with a reason code, cutoff and model version.

Success criteria: reproducible forward-looking evaluation, reconcilable cohorts, explicit consent unknown, and a prospective holdout protocol. No model score or positive campaign effect is required.

Metric tree: source events → valid behavioral records → eligible observed buyers → mature repurchase/non-repurchase → frozen risk ranking → analytically selected candidates. A separate randomized campaign measurement branch reports assigned-customer outcomes.

Proposed requirements: marketing selects capacity and permitted channels; analytics fixes eligibility and leakage controls; CRM confirms identity and permission; finance supplies basket value, margin, incentive and contact costs; an experiment owner freezes assignment and outcomes. These are proposed roles, not historical stakeholder conversations.

Questions for stakeholders: What is reachable and consented? Which campaigns overlap? Are purchases omnichannel? What inventory and category promises are valid? What capacity, margin and guardrails define success? None of those absent answers is invented by this reconstruction.
